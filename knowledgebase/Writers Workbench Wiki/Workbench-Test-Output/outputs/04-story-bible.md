# Story bible

> The engine `chapter.extract-bible` op is still a stub (roadmap F1-2), so this is assembled from the outline's characters + the project's existing `story_bible_v2` world entries.

## Characters (from the outline)

### Dr. Tayak Moyaone
- **arc:** A methodical scientist whose academic training conflicts with growing spiritual experiences as she uncovers each artifact, ultimately learning to balance scholarly rigor with ancestral wisdom
- **role:** Protagonist
- **description:** Contemporary Piscataway archaeologist who discovers her family's sacred burial ground through scientific excavation, bridging academic methodology with spiritual awakening.

### Red Fire Woman (The First Mother)
- **arc:** The foundational ancestor whose copper pendant becomes the spiritual anchor for all subsequent generations, embodying the original connection between family and sacred land
- **role:** Ancestral Guide
- **description:** The earliest ancestor from 100 BC whose copper pendant serves as the spiritual catalyst for Dr. Tayak's visions and connection to her lineage.

### The Ancestors
- **arc:** A collective presence that grows stronger with each artifact discovery, representing the accumulated wisdom and experience of the Piscataway people through triumph and tragedy
- **role:** Dynamic Chorus
- **description:** The voices and spirits of all previous generations who guide Dr. Tayak through her discoveries, becoming more present and powerful as she uncovers their stories.

### Historical Erasure
- **arc:** The persistent threat of cultural annihilation that manifests through colonization, forced assimilation, legal denial, and environmental destruction across centuries
- **role:** Antagonistic Force
- **description:** The systemic forces that have attempted to erase Piscataway culture and history, from colonial displacement to modern denial of tribal recognition.
